Train types

To show which train model runs each departure, the app reads SBB's train formation data. That needs your own free key from opentransportdata.swiss. The key stays on this device.

  1. Sign in at api-manager.opentransportdata.swiss.
  2. Open Train Formation Service and copy your key.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save key.

      Dep.LineDestinationPlatform
      Search for a station to see its departures.

      Live data from transport.opendata.ch (SBB timetable and real-time delays), refreshed every 30 seconds. Enter only a departure station for its board, or both stations for trains between them. Trains leaving within 20 minutes count down to the second. Tap a train to watch it. Times shown are Swiss time. Train types come from SBB's train formation data (set up under Settings); photos from Wikimedia Commons.
