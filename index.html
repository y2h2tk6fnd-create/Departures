Train types

To show which train model runs each departure, the app reads SBB's train formation data. That needs your own free key from opentransportdata.swiss. The key stays on this device.

  1. Sign in at api-manager.opentransportdata.swiss.
  2. Open Train Formation Service and copy your key.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save key.

Live data from transport.opendata.ch (SBB timetable and real-time delays), refreshed every 30 seconds. All times are Swiss time. Train types come from SBB's train formation data; photos from Wikimedia Commons.
